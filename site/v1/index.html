The Programme

A play in three acts

No script, no rehearsal, no two shows alike. Here’s how a performance unfolds.

I.

You ask

Say any story out loud: “a knight who’s afraid of the dark”, “pirates on Mars”. No menu, no buttons, just your voice.

II.

It improvises

An AI narrator, running on LiveKit’s real-time voice agents, invents the tale on the spot and tells it aloud, beat by beat.

III.

The arms perform

Two 6-axis robot arms, one white and one black, act it out with props in their grippers: foam sword, shield, flag, crown.

Heckle anytime.

Shout “make the dragon friendly!” mid-scene. The arms freeze, react, and the story bends around your idea, live.
