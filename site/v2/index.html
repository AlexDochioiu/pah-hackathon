How it works

Three steps. Zero scripts.

It’s improv theatre, but the actors are robot arms and the writer is an AI that never says no.

1

You ask

Say any story out loud: “a knight who’s afraid of the dark”, “pirates on Mars”. No buttons, no menus. Just your voice.

2

It improvises

A LiveKit real-time voice agent invents the story on the spot and narrates it out loud, deciding every move as it goes.

3

The arms act it out

Two 6-axis robot arms, one white and one black, perform every beat with props in their grippers: sword, shield, flag, crown.
