01 / How it works

Three scenes. Zero rehearsals.

From a sentence spoken out loud to a fully acted scene, in seconds.

SC 01INT. Your voice
01

You ask

Say any story out loud: “a knight duels a dragon at midnight”, “the last robot on the moon”. No menu, no buttons.

SC 02INT. The narrator
02

It improvises

A LiveKit real-time voice agent invents the tale on the spot and tells it aloud, deciding beat by beat what each arm should do.

Out of the fog rises a dragon,
eyes like furnaces.

SC 03EXT. The stage
03

The arms act it out

Two 6-axis YAM arms, one white and one black, perform with props in their grippers: foam sword, shield, flag, crown.

● Interrupt anytime

Shout “make the dragon friendly!” mid-sentence. The arms freeze, the narrator deletes the last line, and the scene rewrites itself around your idea, live.
